ABOUT

Where Faith Meets Grace

Shri Bageshwar Dham is a sacred place devoted to Shri Hanuman Balaji where generations of devotees have come with faith and hope. Believed to be over 300 years old, the Dham welcomes thousands of people every day. With their Arzi, devotees come before Balaji Maharaj seeking his blessings, guidance and grace through Pujya Dhirendra Krishna Shastri Ji Maharaj, Shri Bageshwar Dham Sarkar.

✦
~300 Years Old

A living tradition of devotion, tapasya and seva that began with Shri Sannyasi Baba in Gram Gadha, Chhatarpur.

🙏
Thousands Every Week

Every Tuesday and Saturday, devotees gather for Divya Darbar seeking Balaji’s grace. Entry is free for all.

❤️
Seva & Sanatan

Faith here becomes service — Annapurna Rasoi, Kanya Vivah, Cancer Hospital, Vedic Gurukul and Gau Seva.

THE SPIRIT OF THE DHAM

Come Closer to the Divine

Step into the sacred presence of Shri Balaji Maharaj, where faith brings peace and devotion finds purpose. The Dham is not just a temple — it is a living centre of Sanatan Dharma where Katha, Darshan and Seva flow as one.

Whether you come with an Arzi, to attend Divya Darbar, to listen to Katha, or simply to sit in the presence of Balaji, you are welcomed with the same grace that has drawn devotees for centuries from across India and the world.

Located in Village Gadha, Post Ganj, District Chhatarpur, Madhya Pradesh, the Dham continues the unbroken spiritual lineage of Shri Sannyasi Baba and Shri Dadaguruji under the guidance of Pujya Guruji.

What Happens at the Dham
  • Nitya Darshan — Daily darshan of Shri Balaji (approx. 7:30 AM – 9:00 PM)
  • Divya Darbar — Every Tuesday & Saturday from 6:00 AM
  • Vibhuti & Mantra Healing — From 8:00 PM onwards on Darbar days
  • Katha & Pravachan — Sacred narrations by Guruji
  • Seva — Free meals, welfare and community service
Who Comes Here

People from every walk of life — those seeking relief from suffering, those offering gratitude, those wishing to deepen their connection with Sanatan Dharma, and those who simply wish to sit in the presence of Balaji. Devotees travel from every corner of India and from countries abroad, united by faith and hope.
