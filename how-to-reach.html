How to Reach

Village Gadha, Post Ganj, District Chhatarpur, Madhya Pradesh 471105

LOCATION

Plan Your Visit to Bageshwar Dham

The Dham is located in Gram Gadha, Post Ganj, District Chhatarpur, Madhya Pradesh 471105. Entry is free for all. Divya Darbar is held every Tuesday and Saturday. Thousands of devotees visit every week from across India and abroad.

By Air

Nearest airports:
• Khajuraho Airport (approx. 45–55 km)
• Jabalpur Airport (further)

From the airport, hire a taxi or take a bus towards Chhatarpur / Gadha. Private taxis are readily available.

By Train

Nearest major stations:
• Chhatarpur
• Khajuraho
• Satna

From the station, local taxis, autos and buses are available to reach Gram Gadha / Ganj.

By Road

Well connected by road from Chhatarpur, Khajuraho, Jhansi, Sagar and other nearby towns. Regular state and private buses ply to the region. Private vehicles can drive directly to the Dham.
