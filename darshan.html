Come closer to the divine presence of Shri Balaji Maharaj. Free entry for all.

नित्य दर्शन

Daily Darshan of Shri Balaji

Shri Bageshwar Dham welcomes devotees every day for darshan of Shri Hanuman Balaji. Generations have come here with faith and hope. Nitya Darshan is generally available from approximately 7:30 AM to 9:00 PM. Aarti timings (Mangala and Sandhya) vary with seasons and festivals.

Whether you come once or return again and again, the presence of Balaji Maharaj and the atmosphere of devotion leave a lasting impression.

Divya Darbar

Every Tuesday and Saturday. Free entry for all. Divya Darbar begins from 6:00 AM. Abhimantrit Vibhuti distribution and Mantra Healing from 8:00 PM onwards.

The Darbar is a gathering of devotion, prayer and shared faith — a place to bring your Arzi and leave with grace.

Come With a Prayer

Devotees come with their Arzi seeking blessings, guidance and grace of Balaji Maharaj through Pujya Guruji. Some seek strength, some come with gratitude, others simply wish to experience a moment of faith.

Leave With Faith

The Divya Darbar creates a deeply personal experience for every devotee. People from different walks of life leave with renewed faith, peace and a stronger connection to Sanatan Dharma.

Practical Information

  • Entry: Free for all devotees
  • Dress: Modest clothing recommended
  • Arzi: Present your prayer as guided by seva volunteers; see Apply Arzi page for more
  • Live: When you cannot visit in person, join Live Darshan on official YouTube and Facebook
