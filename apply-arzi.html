ARZI

Your Prayer Before Balaji Maharaj

At Shri Bageshwar Dham, devotees present their Arzi (prayer / request) before Shri Balaji Maharaj. Through the grace of Balaji and the guidance of Pujya Dhirendra Krishna Shastri Ji Maharaj — Shri Bageshwar Dham Sarkar — many find peace, strength, direction and relief from physical, mental, worldly and spiritual sufferings.

The Divya Darbar is the sacred space where these prayers are offered. People come from every walk of life, each carrying their own story. Some seek strength, some come with gratitude, while others simply wish to experience a moment of faith and connection.

How to Present Your Arzi
  1. Visit the Dham during Nitya Darshan or especially on Divya Darbar days (Tuesday & Saturday).
  2. Follow the guidance of the seva volunteers and the official process at the Dham for submitting your Arzi.
  3. Come with a pure heart — the Darbar is a place of prayer, not a transaction. Leave with faith.
  4. If you cannot visit in person, stay connected through Live Darshan and official channels for updates and blessings from afar.
  5. For current procedures, special instructions or helpline support, contact the Dham.
Important Notes
  • Entry to Divya Darbar is free for all.
  • Timings: Divya Darbar from 6:00 AM; Vibhuti & Mantra Healing from 8:00 PM on Darbar days.
  • Processes may be updated from time to time — always follow the latest guidance at the Dham or on official websites and channels.
