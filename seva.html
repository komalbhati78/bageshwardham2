SEVA

सेवा ही सनातन है

Where Devotion Doesn’t End at Prayer. It Becomes Service.

WHAT SEVA MEANS

Seva is Sanatan

Faith was never meant to stay inside the temple walls. It is carried into the world — in a meal for someone who has not eaten, the wedding of a daughter whose family could not afford one, the care offered to a patient with nowhere else to turn. Here, Seva is simply what devotion looks like when it gets up and goes to work.

Guruji has often said there is no greater offering than feeding someone who is hungry, and no greater prayer than easing another’s pain. At Bageshwar Dham, worship and service are never far apart. This tradition of giving is nearly three centuries old — begun by Sannyasi Baba and continued under Pujya Guruji.
