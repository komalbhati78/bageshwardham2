WHO IS GURUJI?

A Devotee at Heart. A Voice for a Generation.

Pt. Shri Dhirendra Krishna Shastri Ji is a follower of the sacred lineage initiated by Shri Sannyasi Baba. This unbroken lineage of spiritual attainment (Siddhi) has flowed down through generations, from the most revered Shri Sannyasi Baba and Shri Dadaguruji to Pt. Shri Dhirendra Ji. It serves as a medium to remove the physical, mental, worldly, and spiritual sufferings of the devotees.

Hailed as one of Bharat’s most illustrious Kathakara of holy scriptures, Guruji ignites a profound love for the Divine and awakens the spirit of Sanatan by reciting the sacred Kathas of the Shrimad Bhagavatam, Shri Ram Katha, Shri Hanumant Katha, and Shri Bhaktamal Katha. Beloved among the saints, and the blessed recipient of Shri Bageshwar Hanuman Ji’s divine grace, he is universally known and revered as ‘Shri Bageshwar Dham Sarkar’.

THE JOURNEY

From a Village Temple to Millions of Hearts

Before the crowds, the stages and the cameras, there was a young devotee sitting in a village temple, listening to the stories of the divine.

CHILDHOOD
A Heart Drawn to Hanuman Ji

From a young age, Guruji developed deep devotion to Shri Hanuman Ji. He spent time at the village temple and learned the scriptures with his father and grandfather, shaping the spiritual path ahead.

FIRST KATHA
When Devotion Became a Calling

As a young teenager, Guruji began sharing Katha with others, encouraged by his grandfather. What started as small gatherings grew into a lifelong commitment to bringing the wisdom of the scriptures closer to people.

TRADITION
Carrying a Sacred Tradition Forward

After the passing of his grandfather, Guruji took on the responsibility of carrying forward the spiritual tradition of Bageshwar Dham. With it, his commitment to Sanatan and Seva grew deeper still.

WIDER REACH
A Voice Beyond the Village

From Katha gatherings across Madhya Pradesh, Guruji’s voice reached a wider audience through television, digital platforms and social media — connecting devotees across the country and the world.

DIVYA DARBAR

Come With a Prayer. Leave With Faith.

For countless devotees, the Divya Darbar at Bageshwar Dham is a place to bring their prayers, hopes and heartfelt questions. People come from different walks of life, each carrying their own story. Some seek strength, some come with gratitude, while others simply wish to experience a moment of faith and connection. The Darbar is a gathering of devotion, prayer and shared faith — a deeply personal experience for every devotee who comes.

THE MISSION

A Mission to Serve, Inspire and Uplift

Guruji’s mission is to bring people closer to faith, help them reconnect with their roots, and make the values of Sanatan Dharma a part of everyday life. Through Katha, Seva and meaningful initiatives — Annapurna Rasoi, Kanya Vivah, the Cancer Hospital, Vedic Gurukul, Gau Seva and more — the message goes beyond words and becomes something people can experience in their own lives.

What began in Gadha has grown into a message that reaches people across India and beyond. Through Katha, Seva and digital platforms, Guruji’s teachings continue to connect with people from different generations, communities and parts of the world.
