THE DHAM

History of Bageshwar Dham

From the tapasya of Shri Sannyasi Baba to a living centre of Sanatan Dharma for millions.

ORIGINS

A Tradition Nearly Three Centuries Old

The service and devotion you see today did not begin with a target or a strategy. It began roughly three hundred years ago, when Shri Sannyasi Baba first made this land in Gadha a place of tapasya — and of care for whoever arrived at its doors. Through the unbroken lineage of Shri Dadaguruji and now under Pujya Dhirendra Krishna Shastri Ji Maharaj, that intention continues. The scale has changed; the spirit has not.

Long before the world knew Bageshwar Dham, there was a village, a temple, and a Sannyasi. The holy saint whose Tapasya made the village Gadha a Taposthal. Few know the full story that Guruji carries — about Shri Sannyasi Baba, Dada Guru, and the events that shaped this lineage. Of Guruji’s years, many have been spent in communion with that living tradition.

~300 YEARS AGO

Shri Sannyasi Baba — The Founding Saint

The founding saint whose intense tapasya transformed the village of Gadha into a sacred Taposthal. His presence and grace established the spiritual foundation of Bageshwar Dham. Devotees still speak his name with reverence. The land itself became a place of care — whoever arrived at its doors found shelter and blessing.

THE LINEAGE

Shri Dadaguruji

The sacred spiritual tradition flowed through Shri Dadaguruji, preserving the Siddhi and the values of Sanatan Dharma for the next generation. The first meeting of Shri Dada-Guruji with Shri Sannyasi Baba, and a generation later the present Peethadhishwar’s own connection, form the unbroken parampara that continues today.

1996 ONWARDS

Pt. Dhirendra Krishna Shastri Ji — Peethadhishwar

Born in Gadha, Chhatarpur, Madhya Pradesh, Guruji grew up in the same spiritual soil. From a young age he developed deep devotion to Shri Hanuman Ji, spent time at the village temple, and learned the scriptures with his elders. As a teenager he began sharing Katha. After the passing of his grandfather, he took on the responsibility of carrying forward the tradition of Bageshwar Dham. From small gatherings to millions of hearts across India and the world, his voice has become the guiding light of the Dham.

TODAY

A Living Centre of Faith & Seva

Every Tuesday and Saturday, thousands gather for Divya Darbar. Annapurna Rasoi serves free meals to every visitor. Kanya Vivah has supported over 1,200 daughters. A free cancer hospital is under construction. Vedic Gurukul, Gau Seva and environmental initiatives continue the centuries-old tradition of turning devotion into service. The message that began in a village temple now reaches hearts across the globe.

Mera Sannyasi — Guruji’s Book

In Mera Sannyasi, Pujya Guruji writes in his own words the story of Shri Sannyasi Baba, his Dada Guru, and the parampara that binds them — part biography, part autobiography, entirely an offering of devotion. Available in Hindi and English.
